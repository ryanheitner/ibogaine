Israelis for Ibogaine
ישראלים למען איבוגאין

The Ibogaine Foundation of Israel is a non-profit organization dedicated to advancing ibogaine-assisted therapy for PTSD, TBI, addiction, and neurological conditions. We advocate for evidence-based policy, clinical access, and public education.
