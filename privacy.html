Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 26 August 2026

Israelis for Ibogaine is operated by Iboga Bush (R.A.) / סנה איבוגה (ע”ר), an Israeli non-profit association registered with the Ministry of Justice under number 580841922. This notice explains how information submitted through this website is handled. It applies to israelisforibogaine.org and to the contact and petition forms hosted on it.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form or the petition form you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, city, subject, and message. These are the only fields we ask for, and only the name, email address and message are required. Please do not include medical records, diagnoses, passwords, identity numbers, or other sensitive information in a form submission — if you need to discuss a medical matter, say so briefly and we will suggest a more appropriate channel.

Information collected automatically

This website is a set of static pages. We do not operate advertising trackers or third-party analytics profiling on it. Our hosting provider records standard server request logs, and the video and podcast players embedded on the Media page are loaded only if you click to play them — until you do, no request is made to those third-party services.

How we use information

We use the information you submit to respond to your enquiry, to administer the public petition, to communicate about the organization's activities where you have asked us to, and to keep appropriate organizational records. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not currently accept donations through this website.

Service providers and retention

Form submissions are transmitted to the service used to process the form and may be stored in the organization's records. We limit access to the people who need it for organizational purposes, and we retain information only for as long as is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was given, or as required by Israeli law.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw your name from the petition, by writing to info@israelisforibogaine.org. We will respond within a reasonable period.

External links

This website links to external websites, journals, and social-media services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. We do not control or endorse how those services process information.

Changes to this notice

If we change how information is handled we will update this page and the review date above. Material changes will be noted at the top of the page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@israelisforibogaine.org, or by post to the organization's registered address, available from the Israeli Registrar of Associations under registration number 580841922.
