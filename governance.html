Governance

Our legal status and regulatory strategy

We think an organization asking for a change in national drug policy should be legible about its own governance. Israelis for Ibogaine operates as an Amuta — a non-profit association registered with the Israeli Ministry of Justice — under the Hebrew name סנה איבוגה (ע”ר) and the English name IBOGA BUSH (R.A.), registration number 580841922. We hold a board-approved conflict-of-interest policy and whistleblower policy, and we do not currently accept public donations through this website. This page sets out that legal footing and the three regulatory pathways we are pursuing.

Last reviewed: 26 August 2026

Legal Strategy

A Clear Regulatory Pathway

Entity & Tax Structure

Ibogaine Israel is registered as a formal Amuta (Non-Profit Association) under the Israeli Ministry of Justice. We are not currently accepting donations; any future fundraising and tax treatment will be announced once formally established.

Legal Entity

  • Official Name (Hebrew): סנה איבוגה (ע”ר)
  • Official Name (English): IBOGA BUSH (R.A.)
  • Registration Number: 580841922
  • Registered Authority: Israeli Registrar of Associations (Rasham Ha’Amutot)

The website brand name may differ from the legal registered entity name. All operations, activities, and services are conducted under the registered amutah listed above.

1. Reclassify Ibogaine

Transitioning from Schedule 1 to 'Controlled Medical Substance' enabling supervised clinical and compassionate use within licensed facilities.

2. National Pilot Programme

Advocating for a government-sanctioned 'National Ibogaine Pilot' specifically targeting combat veterans and Nova massacre survivors.

3. Compassionate Use Status

Fast-tracking MoH approval for severe, treatment-resistant patients while full trial data is compiled.
